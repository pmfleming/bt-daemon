## 1. Bounded, linear vendor framing

`src/framing.rs` now scans each incoming byte once, retains only a bounded encoded
body, and reuses that buffer. Previously it appended the entire input and repeatedly
drained/searched a vector; complete oversized frames bypassed the incomplete-frame
limit. The limit now applies regardless of fragmentation/coalescing. The existing
boundary (opening delimiter plus body <= 4096 bytes) is preserved. Shared HDLC
flags, Sony empty-frame rejection, and noise/corruption recovery remain supported.

One shared boundary matrix covers both delimiter styles, four lengths around the
limit, three chunk sizes, and recovery. Removed duplicated vendor-specific size
checks; retained their independent wire vectors and payload validation tests.

Validation: 67 unit + 2 integration tests passed, one existing ignored experiment;
Clippy with `-D warnings`, formatting and diff checks passed. RQLens reports
`DelimitedFrames::push` Halstead effort 49,535 -> 35,652. Its explicit bounded-state
handling increases cognitive/cyclomatic complexity from 6/5 to 11/7; this stage
prioritizes a real resource-bound defect and linear processing, not lower branch
counts at the expense of validation. No throughput benchmark is claimed.
