Ranked findings and implemented changes
1. Repeated socket/D-Bus fixture setup and JSON reply decoding were the clearest
   actionable duplication. Share owned test proxies and typed RPC decoding;
   borrow D-Bus JSON parameters, move socket responses rather than cloning them,
   and propagate fixture I/O failures. Retain transport parity, owner loss,
   cancellation, private status, atomic snapshots and recreation assertions.
   Production Service now owns its common exported-interface lookup as well.
2. RSSI management reply correlation ranked near the production complexity
   ceiling (cognitive 9). Match the successful payload's peer and shape together,
   retaining adapter/opcode/event/declared-length/status guards. Consolidate
   rejected datagrams into one transport table; add positive classic RSSI,
   signed -128 LE and consistently length-prefixed oversized replies.
3. Outgoing OBEX owner cleanup cloned every matching ID into a temporary Vec,
   then performed another lookup. HashMap::extract_if moves only owned entries
   directly. New table checks multiple same-owner transfers, foreign/unowned
   isolation, missing owners, idempotence and actual cancellation delivery.
4. Trusted Fast Pair catalog parsing allocated a Vec for every three-byte ID.
   Decode directly into its fixed array. Extend existing tests through real
   catalog loading: absent/private/writable/oversized/nonregular files, duplicate
   normalized IDs and invalid widths. Advertisement tables add independent flag
   combinations, repeated unknown fields and zero-length unknown rejection.
   Its production rewrite was reverted after measurements showed no benefit.
5. Larger orchestration functions (snapshot assembly, daemon startup and
   subscription forwarding) remain important effort hotspots. Their ownership,
   acknowledgement and retry boundaries did not justify fragmentation here.
   The AST clone matches include unrelated small classification/shutdown bodies;
   they were not forcibly unified. No production dead item was proven/removable;
   no dead-code improvement is claimed. Strict compiler/Clippy checks are clean.
